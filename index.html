Pain Relief Patches

Natural patches designed to relieve pain for women during menstruation, after childbirth, and during breastfeeding. They are made from natural ingredients like jasmine and frankincense, providing effective comfort without harmful chemicals, making them safe for regular use.

Soft and safe diapers designed for babies’ delicate skin. They help reduce skin irritation and provide extra comfort using gentle, natural materials.
